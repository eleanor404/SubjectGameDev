Communities and Competitions

Learning game development is more enjoyable when you share ideas, collaborate with others, and participate in creative challenges. The communities and competitions below are free to join and provide opportunities to practice programming, receive feedback, and showcase your projects.

Free Communities and Competitions

  • Scratch Community Share games and animations, remix projects created by others, participate in community studios, and receive feedback from creators around the world.
  • Games for Change Student Challenge A game design competition where students create digital games that explore real-world issues and positive social change.
  • Global Game Jam An annual worldwide event where participants work together to design and develop games based on a shared theme.
  • itch.io Game Jams Join free online game development competitions that encourage creativity, experimentation, and collaboration for developers of all skill levels.
  • CoderDojo Find free coding clubs where young people can learn programming, build projects, and develop digital skills with volunteer mentors.
  • Hour of Code Explore free coding activities and tutorials that introduce programming concepts through games, puzzles, and interactive projects.

Why Join a Community?

Participating in communities and competitions can help you:

  • Practice programming and game design through real projects and creative challenges.
  • Receive constructive feedback from experienced developers, educators, and other learners.
  • Discover new ideas, techniques, and tools by exploring games created by the community.
  • Build confidence by sharing your work and collaborating with others who enjoy creating games.
