Organizations

Many nonprofit organizations and educational programs provide free opportunities to learn programming, game design, digital creativity, and computer science. These organizations offer lessons, workshops, competitions, teaching resources, and community programs that support learners of all ages and backgrounds.

Free Organizations and Educational Programs

  • Code.org A nonprofit organization that provides free computer science curriculum, coding activities, and professional learning resources for students and educators.
  • Games for Change Promotes the creation of games that inspire learning, civic engagement, and positive social impact through educational programs and student challenges.
  • Endless Foundation Develops free technology, educational software, and digital learning resources to improve access to computing around the world.
  • Urban Arts Offers programs that combine game design, digital media, and computer science to support creative learning for students and educators.
  • Computer Science Teachers Association (CSTA) Supports computer science educators through professional development, classroom resources, and a global community of teachers.
  • Girls Who Code Provides free clubs, programs, and learning opportunities that encourage girls to explore computer science and technology careers.
  • Black Girls Code Empowers Black girls through workshops, coding education, and technology programs that build confidence and digital skills.
  • The Scratch Foundation Supports the development of Scratch and promotes creative learning through coding, storytelling, and collaborative digital projects.

Why Explore These Organizations?

These organizations can help learners:

  • Discover free lessons, activities, and educational resources for learning programming and game development.
  • Participate in workshops, clubs, competitions, and community events that encourage creativity and collaboration.
  • Connect with mentors, educators, and other learners who share an interest in technology and digital creativity.
  • Explore pathways into computer science, game design, and technology careers while building practical skills.
