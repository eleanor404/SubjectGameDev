Libraries

Digital libraries provide free access to books, research articles, educational resources, and scholarly publications. These collections are excellent starting points for learning about game development, computer science, programming, digital media, and educational technology.

Free Digital Libraries

  • Open Library Borrow millions of free digital books, including titles on programming, game design, computer science, and technology.
  • Internet Archive Access a large collection of digitized books, software, manuals, magazines, and historical computing resources.
  • Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ) Search thousands of peer-reviewed, open-access academic journals across many subjects, including education, technology, and computer science.
  • ERIC Digital Library Search education research, journal articles, reports, and teaching resources published by the Education Resources Information Center.
  • CORE Research Repository Discover millions of open-access research papers collected from universities and research institutions around the world.
  • arXiv Browse free research papers and preprints covering computer science, artificial intelligence, mathematics, engineering, and related fields.

Using Digital Libraries Effectively

When searching for information about game development, try using keywords such as:

  • Game design and game development to find books and research about creating games.
  • Programming, Python, or computer science to learn coding skills used in game development.
  • Educational games or game-based learning to explore how games are used in classrooms and learning environments.
  • Look for resources published recently to learn about current game engines, programming tools, and industry practices.
